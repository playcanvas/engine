Jet Fighter Terrain Textures
============================

The ground textures of the jet fighter example - scanned materials from Poly Haven, released into
the public domain (CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each is
the 1k JPG of the diffuse color and of the OpenGL normal map, unchanged.

aerial_grass_rock_diff_1k.jpg, aerial_grass_rock_nor_1k.jpg
    "Aerial Grass Rock" by Rob Tuytel - https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock

sparse_grass_diff_1k.jpg, sparse_grass_nor_1k.jpg
    "Sparse Grass" by Amal Kumar - https://polyhaven.com/a/sparse_grass

dirt_aerial_02_diff_1k.jpg, dirt_aerial_02_nor_1k.jpg
    "Dirt Aerial 02" by Rob Tuytel - https://polyhaven.com/a/dirt_aerial_02

aerial_rocks_02_diff_1k.jpg, aerial_rocks_02_nor_1k.jpg
    "Aerial Rocks 02" by Rob Tuytel - https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_rocks_02

aerial_beach_01_diff_1k.jpg, aerial_beach_01_nor_1k.jpg
    "Aerial Beach 01" by Rob Tuytel - https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_beach_01

rock_face_03_diff_1k.jpg, rock_face_03_nor_1k.jpg (the OpenGL normal map, renamed)
    "Rock Face 03" by Dario Barresi (photography) and Rico Cilliers (processing) -
    https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_03
