Lens flare element textures: glows, irises, a halo, spikes, diffraction bursts, streaks and
spectral smudges, 512x512 PNGs with the shape in the colour channels and its coverage in alpha.

Generated procedurally by a numpy script, generate.py, attached to the pull request which added them.
It documents each shape, regenerates them, and with --all writes further variants.

Created for the PlayCanvas Engine and released under its MIT license.
