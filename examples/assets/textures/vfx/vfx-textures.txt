These textures are procedurally generated for the PlayCanvas examples by utils/generate-vfx-textures.mjs,
and contain no third party content:

* smoke-flipbook.webp - 8x8 frames of a billowing smoke puff with baked lighting, straight alpha.
* explosion-flipbook.webp - 8x8 frames of a fireball cooling into smoke, straight alpha.
* scorch-decal.webp - a scorch mark.
* bullet-hole-decal.webp - a bullet hole with cracks.
* rune-decal.webp - a magic rune circle.
